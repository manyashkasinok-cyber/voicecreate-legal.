VoiceCreate Privacy Policy
Last updated: October 12, 2026
This policy explains what data VoiceCreate (the "Bot") collects, why, how long it is kept, and what your rights are. It supplements our Terms of Service.
1. Data controller
Zan, contact: zenkosquare@gmail.com · Telegram: @zanlov · Support server: https://discord.gg/aVwxp9dC7u
2. Data we process
The Bot only collects what it needs to work. It does not ask for your real name, address, phone number or payment details.
2.1 Discord identifiers
Numeric IDs of users, servers, channels and roles, needed to link data to the right person or server. Display names and avatars are read to render cards (profile, leaderboard, level); they are not stored long-term.
2.2 Activity and progression
• XP, level, time spent in voice channels (in seconds), number of messages sent (a simple counter) and last-activity date;
• achievements, badges, missions, weekly statistics, prestige, character and upgrades;
• mini-game statistics (games played, wins, losses, streaks) and personal pet.
2.3 Virtual economy
Virtual coin balance, aggregated casino win/loss history, items, chests and shop purchases. These have no monetary value.
2.4 Voice channels and presets
Owner of a temporary channel, member limit, locked or hidden state, 24/7 options, personal presets (lists of allowed members, moderators and auto-invite member IDs), and saved roles when a server enables this feature.
2.5 VIP status
VIP type, grant date, duration, expiry date and cumulative amount spent on the subscription. The Bot does not process payments: no banking data is collected or stored by it.
2.6 Server configuration
IDs of channels, categories, roles and administrators, command prefix, log, shop and AutoMod settings, defined by each server's administrators.
2.7 Message content
The Bot needs to read messages to detect its prefixed commands (e.g. a prefix followed by a command) and mentions of the Bot. It does not store the content of your messages: it only counts them (see 2.2). For commands, a short excerpt (the first 40 characters) may appear in a technical diagnostic log (see 2.8). Direct messages are not read: the Bot only sends DMs triggered by a feature (for example a voice channel invitation, or a notification related to a command you triggered).
2.8 Technical logs
The Bot writes diagnostic logs (errors, Discord API slowness, button response times, command excerpts) to fix bugs. They are size-limited and regularly rotated. Server administrators may also enable logs on their own server (voice activity, levels, VIP, admin actions): these logs are posted in channels the administrator chooses and controls.

4. Sharing of data
• We do not sell your data and do not share it for commercial purposes.
• Some information is visible by design: your level, XP or rank appear in leaderboards and cards on the server where you use the Bot, and administrators of that server see the logs they have enabled.
• Discord Inc. processes data transiting through its platform under its own Privacy Policy.
• Our hosting provider hosts the Bot's database: MobaXterm France.
• We may disclose data if required by law or by a competent authority.
5. Retention
• Progression, economy and settings data: kept as long as the Bot is in use. Deletion after 12 months of inactivity.
• Server data: kept as long as the Bot is present on the server. After removal, the server configuration is deleted on request or within a few days.
• Automatic database backups: one per day, the last 7 are kept and then overwritten. A deletion is therefore effective in backups at most 7 days later.
• Technical logs: size-limited files, rotated automatically.
• VIP data: kept for the duration of the subscription, then for as long as needed for possible accounting or dispute obligations.
6. Your rights
If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom (and, depending on your region, other jurisdictions), you have the right to:
• access your data and obtain a copy;
• rectify inaccurate data;
• erase your data (reset your profile);
• restrict or object to processing based on legitimate interests;
• data portability;
• lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in France: the CNIL, www.cnil.fr).
To exercise these rights, write to zenkosquare@gmail.com (or message @zanlov on Telegram, or ask on the support server) with your Discord ID. We reply within one month. We may ask you to prove you own the account.
7. Security
Data is stored in a local SQLite database on a server with restricted access. The Bot's authentication token is kept outside the code. No system is infallible, so we cannot guarantee absolute security; in case of a data breach likely to put you at risk, we will inform you and the competent authority as required by law.
8. Transfers outside the European Union
Discord Inc. is based in the United States. Our hosting provider may be located in EU: France. Where applicable, transfers rely on appropriate safeguards (for example the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision).
9. Children
The Bot is intended for people who meet Discord's minimum age (13, or higher depending on the country; 15 in France to consent alone to data processing). We do not knowingly collect data beyond what is described here. If you believe a child under the required age has provided us data, contact us so we can delete it.
10. Removing the Bot and deleting your data
• You can stop using the Bot at any time; administrators can remove it from their server.
• To have your personal data deleted (profile, levels, economy, presets), contact us as described in section 6.
• Administrators can request deletion of their server's configuration.
11. Changes
We may update this policy. The date of the latest update appears at the top. Significant changes will be announced via the support server or the Bot's changelog.
